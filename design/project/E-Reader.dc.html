Backfill is throttled

The backfill now runs through a bounded queue instead of spawning one task per cam, so a fresh library never has more than 4 yt-dlp processes alive.

  • Concurrency: buffer_unordered(CONCURRENCY); nothing is spawned up front.
  • 429s: HTTP Error 429 on stderr becomes FetchError::RateLimited; the loop waits with a doubling backoff.
  • Progress: "Fetching metadata · 212 of 1,729" through the existing progress bar.
