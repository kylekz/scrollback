The backfill now runs through a bounded queue, so a fresh library never has more than 4 yt-dlp processes alive.

  • 429s: HTTP Error 429 on stderr becomes FetchError::RateLimited; the loop backs off, doubling.
  • Progress: "Fetching metadata · 212 of 1,729" through the existing progress bar.
